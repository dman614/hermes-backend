## The Better Way: Call Forwarding (Keep Bob's Number)

### How Call Forwarding Works

```
FLOW:
  Customer calls: 250-555-1234 (Bob's real number)
         ↓
  Bob's phone carrier routes to: 250-555-PLUMB (Hermes)
         ↓
  Hermes AI answers
         ↓
  Customer talks to AI
         ↓
  AI captures everything
         ↓
  Saves to database
         ↓
  Sends SMS to Bob (notifies him)
         ↓
  Bob calls customer back or assigns to tech

FROM CUSTOMER PERSPECTIVE:
  They always call 250-555-1234 (Bob's number)
  They don't know it's routed to Hermes
  Everything is seamless
```

## Setup Process: 3 Simple Steps

### Step 1: Bob Calls His Phone Provider (20 minutes)

**Scenario: Bob uses Bell Canada for his business line**

```
Bob calls Bell: "I want to set up call forwarding"

Bell asks:
  1. "Which number? 250-555-1234"
  2. "Forward to where? Hermes number (250-555-PLUMB)"
  3. "When? All calls (or only after hours, or only unanswered)"

Options Bob can choose:
  A) Forward ALL calls immediately
  B) Forward after 3 rings (let Bob answer first, then forward)
  C) Forward only after business hours
  D) Forward only unanswered calls
  E) Simultaneous ring (both Bob's phone + Hermes ring at same time)
```

**Option A: Forward ALL Calls (Best for Automation)**
```
Customer calls: 250-555-1234
Bell immediately routes to: 250-555-PLUMB
Hermes AI answers: "Thanks for calling Bob's Plumbing"
Bob sees SMS: "Emergency call captured"

Bob's actual phone: Doesn't ring (unless he adds simultaneous ring)
Bob's business number: Still his "real" number (unchanged)
Customers: Don't know anything changed (seamless)
```

**Option E: Simultaneous Ring (Best for Control)**
```
Customer calls: 250-555-1234
Bell rings BOTH:
  - Bob's personal cell (rings for 3 seconds)
  - Hermes AI (rings immediately)
  
Bob can:
  - Answer immediately if available: Handles call personally
  - Ignore: Hermes AI picks up automatically
  
Bob sees SMS either way: Either he's on call with customer or AI captured

Advantage: Bob has choice (answer fast calls personally, route others to AI)
```

### Q: "What if Hermes goes down? Will calls be lost?"

**A: No. Multiple safeguards:**

```

  - Hermes runs on 99.9% uptime infrastructure
  - Multiple servers, automatic failover
  - If one fails, others take over

Safeguard 2: Fallback routing
  - If Hermes unavailable, calls revert to Bob's voicemail
  - Customer leaves message
  - Bob gets voicemail (same as before)
  - Bob can call back

Safeguard 3: Alert system
  - If Hermes goes down, Bob gets alert
  - Bob can manually disable forwarding (one call)
  - Resume normal operation

Reality: Less likely to fail than any single system
  - Hermes: 99.9% uptime
  - Your old system: Maybe 90% uptime
  - Both together: 99.99% uptime
```
