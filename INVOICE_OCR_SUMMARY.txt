================================================================================
THE PROBLEM
================================================================================

Bob receives supplier invoice (Home Depot, Rona, etc.)

Current workflow:
  1. Opens email attachment or scans paper (5 min)
  2. Opens QuickBooks
  3. Creates new Bill
  4. Manually types: vendor, invoice #, date, due date, line items, total
  5. Categorizes expense
  6. Saves to QB
  7. Sets reminder (usually forgets)
  8. Pays invoice late
  9. Marks paid in QB

Total time: 15-30 minutes per invoice
Problem: Manual errors, missed deadlines, disorganized

================================================================================
THE SOLUTION: INTELLIGENT INVOICE OCR & AUTOMATION
================================================================================

Bob's new workflow:

1. UPLOAD (30 seconds)
   - Takes photo/scans invoice
   - Uploads to Hermes

2. OCR EXTRACTION (Automatic)
   - AWS Textract extracts text (99% accuracy)
   - Parser extracts vendor, amount, date, items
   - Confidence scores calculated

3. QB AUTO-SYNC (One click)
   - Bob approves extracted data
   - Hermes creates Bill in QB automatically

4. PAYMENT REMINDERS (Automatic)
   - 3 days before: "Payment due in 3 days"
   - On due date: "DUE TODAY"
   - If overdue: "OVERDUE X days"

5. MARK PAID (One tap)
   - Bob pays invoice
   - Taps "Mark as Paid" in Hermes
   - QB Bill updates to "Paid"
   - Reminders stop

TOTAL TIME: 30 seconds (vs 15-30 minutes)
TIME SAVED: 14.5-29.5 minutes per invoice

DATABASE SCHEMA
================================================================================

Created tables:

  - id, contractor_id, supplier_id
  - vendor_name, invoice_number, invoice_date, due_date
  - ocr_raw_text, ocr_confidence, extraction_method
  - subtotal, tax, total_amount
  - payment_status, payment_date, paid_amount, payment_method
  - quickbooks_bill_id, quickbooks_sync_status, quickbooks_synced_at
  - reminder_sent_day_before, reminder_sent_due_date, reminder_sent_overdue
  - notes, category, status

supplier_invoice_items:
  - id, invoice_id, description, quantity, unit_price, line_total
  - expense_category, part_id, quickbooks_item_id
  - confidence scores for each field

invoice_payment_reminders:
  - id, invoice_id, contractor_id
  - reminder_type (Before_Due, On_Due, Overdue)
  - reminder_date, is_sent, sent_at, sent_via
  - delivery_status
