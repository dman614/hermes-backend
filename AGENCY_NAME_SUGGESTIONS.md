# Agency Name Suggestions for Your Home Services AI Platform

**Context About Your Business:**
- Target: Home services contractors (plumbing, HVAC, electrical)
- Location: Comox Valley + Campbell River, BC
- Value prop: AI phone answering + instant quotes + scheduling + billing
- Positioning: "HighLevel for Trades" (specialist for contractors, not horizontal)
- Approach: Data-driven, honest market research, execution-focused, no fluff
- Philosophy: Agents + tools (not more agents), self-hosting when it makes sense, long-term value

**Naming Strategy:**
1. Should communicate "AI for trades"
2. Should feel local (BC, Canada)
3. Should be memorable (short, easy to spell)
4. Should avoid generic SaaS names
5. Should hint at automation/agents without being cliché

---

## Tier 1: Strong Candidates (My Recommendations)

### 1. **Hermes** ✅
**Why it already works:**
- You're already using "Hermes" as the platform name
- Hermes = messenger god (fitting for call handling)
- Short, memorable, professional
- Works as both platform name AND agency name
- No confusion between platform and service

**Example marketing:**
- "Hermes handles your calls. Hermes manages your jobs. Hermes runs your business."
- Brand tagline: "Hermes for Home Services" or just "Hermes"

**Concerns:**
- Might feel too generic (other companies use Hermes)
- Less "local" to BC
- Consider trademark search first

**Recommendation:** Strong default choice. If you rebrand, stays the same.

---

### 2. **ToolChain** (or Tool Chain)
**Why it fits:**
- Describes your core architecture (agents + tools chaining together)
- Implies "proven methodology" (chain of command)
- Contractor-friendly (they speak "tools")
- Memorable, short, unique

**Example marketing:**
- "Your complete toolchain for home services"
- "From calls to invoices. One toolchain."
- Logo: Wrench + chain imagery

**Fit:**
- ✅ Contractor-friendly language
- ✅ Technically accurate (LangChain agents + tools)
- ✅ Unique (not many using this)
- ✅ Scalable (works from 1 to 1,000 contractors)

**Concerns:**
- Might need explaining to non-technical contractors
- "ToolChain" is somewhat technical

**Recommendation:** Strong fit if you want contractor vocabulary.

## What NOT to Name It

❌ **Generic SaaS names:**
- "ProManager", "SmartPro", "AutoPro" (too generic)

❌ **Too cute/trendy:**
- "TaskZap", "QuoteNinja", "JobHero" (feels unprofessional)

❌ **Overused in home services:**
- "ServiceXYZ", "JobXYZ", "ProXYZ" (low differentiation)

❌ **Too technical:**
- "LangChain for Trades", "Agent Pro" (confuses contractors)

❌ **Regional lock-in (if scaling nationally):**
- "Vancouver Automation", "BC Services" (limits growth story)

## Action: How to Choose

### Step 1: Test with Contractors (Week 5-6, during beta)
When you have 2-3 beta contractors using the system:
- Ask: "What would you call this service?"
- Show them your top 3 names
- Get honest feedback
- Don't lead them ("do you like X?"), just present options

### Step 2: Check Trademark
For your top choice:
- Google search: "[Name] home services"
- Canadian trademark office (ised.ised-isde.canada.ca)
- US trademark (if expanding south later)
- Domain availability (.ca, .com)

### Step 3: Brand Test (Week 6)
- Logo sketches (2-3 variations)
- Tagline test
- Show to 5-10 contractors
- See which resonates

### Step 4: Decide (Week 7)
- Launch with chosen name
- Consistent branding across all materials
- Website, business cards, LinkedIn, email
