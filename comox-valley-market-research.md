## Part 5: Product Positioning

### Your Unique Angle
**NOT:** "We're like Jobber but cheaper."
**YES:** "We're the AI co-pilot for Comox Valley trades. Your AI answers calls, writes quotes, schedules jobs. You approve it. No manual data entry. No spreadsheets."

### Messaging
- **Time Saved:** 8–10 hours/week on scheduling, quoting, follow-ups
- **Revenue Impact:** Fewer missed calls = captured jobs worth $5–10K each
- **Ease:** Works with your existing phone, email, calendar. No training needed.

### Pricing Logic
- Jobber/HouseCall Pro: $39–349/mo (workflow tool you manage)
- **You:** $299–799/mo (AI does the work, you approve)
- Positioned as **premium for automation**, not budget alternative

## Red Flags to Watch

1. **Contractors won't return calls:** Try email + LinkedIn instead. Or ask Chamber for warm intro.
2. **They're happy with current system:** Dig into pain: "Walk me through a typical day. How long does scheduling take?" Usually reveals 5–8 hr/week waste.
3. **"We're too small":** Small contractors have the most to gain. Saving 5 hrs/week = $500–1K/mo at their labor rate.
4. **Price resistance:** Lead with value ("You're losing $50K/year to missed calls"), not cost ("Only $299/mo").
