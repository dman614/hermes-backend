## The Strategy Behind This Package

### Why Slides Work
- **Engagement**: Interactive keeps attention
- **Pacing**: You control the story flow
- **Objection handling**: Each slide answers a potential doubt
- **Visual credibility**: Looks professional = trust

### Why One-Pager Works
- **Busy contractors**: "TL;DR" format
- **Email friendly**: Sharable, sendable, printable
- **Reference material**: They can review later
- **No learning curve**: Clear sections, easy to scan

### Why Email Works
- **Scaled outreach**: Send to 50 contractors in an hour
- **Trackable**: See who engages
- **Conversations starter**: Gives them a reason to reply
- **Low friction**: They engage on their schedule
