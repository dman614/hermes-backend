## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Issue: "AI got the address wrong"
**Root cause:** Customer mumbled or had accent
**Fix:** Bob reviews transcription, corrects, AI learns
**Prevention:** AI asks to confirm: "Did you say 123 Main Street?"
**Resolution time:** 5 minutes

### Issue: "Quote too high compared to competitor"
**Root cause:** Pricing not aligned with local market
**Fix:** Adjust markup or labor rate in Hermes
**Prevention:** Research local pricing before onboarding
**Resolution time:** 2 minutes to adjust, takes effect immediately

### Issue: "Tech doesn't use the app"
**Root cause:** Tech likes old way or intimidated by tech
**Fix:** Personal training call, show how it helps them
**Prevention:** Emphasize: "App helps YOU, not Bob spying"
**Resolution time:** 1 week (learning curve)

### Issue: "Integration not syncing to QuickBooks"
**Root cause:** OAuth token expired or permissions wrong
**Fix:** Re-authorize connection in settings
**Prevention:** Monthly check of integration status
**Resolution time:** 10 minutes

### Issue: "Customer payment didn't go through"
**Root cause:** Card expired or insufficient funds
**Fix:** Customer re-enters payment method
**Prevention:** Show declined card immediately, not later
**Resolution time:** 5 minutes (customer retries)
