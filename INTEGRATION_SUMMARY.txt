COMMON QUESTIONS FROM CONTRACTORS
================================================================================

Q: "Won't customers be upset with an AI?"
A: No. Customers prefer instant response over long wait. 
   Research: 73% prefer AI over voicemail.
   Reality: Customers love it because you call them back in 20 min.

Q: "What if the AI gets it wrong?"
A: Escalates to you for review. You approve before it matters.
   Accuracy: 85-90% capture rate is standard.
   Fallback: Always backed by human (you) review.

Q: "What if I prefer my current way?"
A: That's fine. Hermes works alongside your current system.
   But: After 1 week, you'll prefer digital (it's faster).
   Common: Week 1 skeptical → Week 3 can't imagine going back.

Q: "Is this expensive?"
A: $500/month pays for itself in 3-4 weeks from missed call recovery.
   Compared: QuickBooks $300 (just accounting), Hermes $500 (everything).
   ROI: 1,600-3,000% in Year 1.

Q: "Will my techs actually use the app?"
A: Yes. It makes their life easier (less boss calls, more autonomy).
   Training: 30 minutes per tech to full proficiency.
   Adoption: 9/10 techs get it immediately.

Q: "What if it breaks?"
A: 99.9% uptime SLA + automatic backups = more reliable than your system.
   Comparison: Your Google Calendar breaks? You lose everything.
               Hermes breaks? Data is backed up and restored in minutes.
