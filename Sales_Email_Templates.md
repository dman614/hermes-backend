## Pro Tips for Email Outreach

1. **Personalize**: Use their first name, reference their specific trade (plumbing, HVAC, electrical)
2. **Lead with value, not features**: "Stop losing $2K per missed call" beats "AI phone answering system"
3. **Short emails**: 3-4 sentences. People skim.
4. **One CTA per email**: Either book a demo, click a link, or reply. Don't give 3 options.
5. **Social proof**: Use contractor names (with permission) from their area
6. **Focus on ROI**: Always show the $ impact. "Save 20 hours/week" is abstract. "$2,400/month" is concrete.
7. **Urgency without pressure**: "30-day free trial" creates urgency without being pushy
8. **Respond fast**: If they reply, answer within 2 hours

## Bottom Line for Sales

- **Lead with ROI** (not features)
- **Show them money** (the problem costs $500-2K per call)
- **Remove friction** (free trial, no credit card, 45-min setup)
- **Use social proof** (other local contractors)
- **Respect their time** (short emails, clear CTAs, quick demos)
- **Follow up** (most conversions happen on attempt 3-5, not 1)

**Remember:** You're not selling software. You're selling recovered revenue, time, and peace of mind.
