================================================================================

Date: September 5, 2026
Impact: Changes onboarding, marketing, and value prop

================================================================================
WHAT WAS WRONG
================================================================================

Original Approach:
  "Get a new Hermes business number (250-555-PLUMB)"
  
Problems:
  ❌ Customers don't know new number
  ❌ Bob has to advertise new number everywhere
  ❌ Old number still gets calls (split effort)
  ❌ Loses 10-year brand identity
  ❌ Business cards need reprinting
  ❌ Website needs updating
  ❌ Google Business needs updating
  ❌ Yelp needs updating
  ❌ High friction for adoption
  ❌ Customer confusion

Real impact:
  "Why are you giving me a new number? I've had 250-555-1234 for 10 years!"
  Customer resistance = adoption failure

================================================================================
THE BETTER WAY
================================================================================

Call Forwarding Approach:
  "Keep your existing business number.
   Set up call forwarding to Hermes.
   AI answers in your name.
   Customers never know it changed."

Advantages:
  ✅ Customer calls 250-555-1234 (same as always)
  ✅ Call forwards to Hermes (transparent)
  ✅ Hermes AI answers as Bob's Plumbing
  ✅ Bob gets SMS alert
  ✅ Zero brand loss
  ✅ Zero updates needed anywhere
  ✅ Zero customer confusion
  ✅ Seamless adoption

Setup:
  1. Bob calls provider: "Forward 250-555-1234 to Hermes"
  2. Provider sets it up: 10 minutes
  3. Cost: $2-5/month
  4. Done. No other changes.

How it works:
  Customer dials: 250-555-1234
         ↓
  Provider routes to: Hermes number (internal)
         ↓
  Hermes AI answers: "Thanks for calling Bob's Plumbing!"
  
  From customer perspective: Called Bob, got instant service (better)
  From Bob's perspective: AI handled it, Bob got SMS (automation)
  From Hermes perspective: Call captured, tracked, monetized

IMPLEMENTATION
================================================================================

For Bob (Contractor):
  1. Calls his phone provider (Bell, Rogers, Telus, etc.)
  2. Says: "I want to forward my business line to another number"
  3. Provides: Existing number (250-555-1234) and Hermes number
  4. Provider sets it up: <10 minutes
  5. Pays: $2-5/month forwarding fee
  6. Result: Done. Calls now forward automatically.

For Hermes (Backend):
  1. Receives call to Hermes number from forwarded call
  2. Knows it came from 250-555-1234 (via Caller ID)
  3. Displays to customer as: 250-555-1234
  4. AI answers in Bob's name
  5. Captures everything
  6. Sends SMS to Bob from his original number

For Customer:
  1. Dials 250-555-1234 (same as always)
  2. Gets connected to Hermes AI (transparent)
  3. Gets better service (instant answer)
  4. Never knows anything changed
